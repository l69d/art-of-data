# Which World Are You In? v4: the driver's seat

**Date:** 2026-09-24. Builds on v3 (`2026-09-24-which-world-v3-stories.md`); engine,
palette, plates and sources are unchanged. User direction: put the viewer in the
driver's seat, make it longer and more immersive, add more art and more story cards that
build up to each big decision. Approved ("continue and don't stop until we reach a
masterpiece").

## Story cards

- The margin shows **one card at a time**, replacing the accumulating column.
- A card is a timestamp or place line (italic) plus one to three lines of narration at
  22 px, then an action.
- A hairline progress rule at the top of the margin fills as the story advances.
- Each card cues a camera move or scene change, so stories are told in shots.
- Actions:
  - **Continue** (click or Space).
  - **Choose** (options, never picked by Space).
  - **Hold** (press and hold a button or Space to make something happen, such as raising
    a fortune).
  - **Do** (click several times, such as eating breakfast).
- Each story runs 6 to 10 cards: build-up, decision, consequence, then a final
  "Moral of the story" card.

## Driving

- **Drag to look:** dragging on any story scene turns the camera, a yaw of ±180° and a
  pitch of ±0.35 rad, on top of the slow drift. It resets on entering a plate and never
  affects flat charts.
- **One hands-on action per story:**
  - I: open the gates (fans stream into the stands); hold to raise the richest
    guest's fortune.
  - II: eat breakfast on the first mornings; then let the days pass.
  - III: turn the damaged bomber over before the general asks.
  - IV: open the five branch reports.
  - V: answer the phone.
  - VI: open Sarah's letter.

## More art (engraved, three.js props)

- **I:** floodlight towers and pitch markings; fans stream in through the gates.

- **III:** spinning propellers; the ghost formation flies off.
- **IV:** five report sheets fanned in space.
- **V:** a ministry façade with camera flashes outside.
- **VI:** a figure drawn in dots holding a letter.
