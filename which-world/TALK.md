# Talk: Which world are you in? (about 3 minutes)

Open `index.html?talk` fullscreen. Let a judge click the choices; you press Space to
continue. The script plays plates I, III and V, then shows the record.

## 0:00 Open (10 s)

*Title plate, with the stadium turning slowly.*

> "Six short stories about data. In each one you sit in someone's seat and make a call.
> Let's see what your gut does."

## 0:10 The stadium (55 s)

*Plate I. Read the engineer's seat aloud and hand the judge the choice.*

> "You're the engineer. The heaviest person imaginable just bought the last ticket.
> Reinforce the stand, turn them away, or let them in?"

Reveal: **0.5%**. "The stand never noticed."

> "Later that night, the same thousand are at your charity gala, and the richest person
> on Earth walks in. Where do you spend your evening?"

Let the red column rise out of the top of the frame: **99.9%**, 18 km tall at this scale.

> "Taleb calls these two worlds Mediocristan and Extremistan. Your gut used one rule for
> both."

Press "Draw it on a log scale": the thousand come back. "Draw it better and half the
insight is free."

## 1:05 The graveyard (50 s)

*Plate III.*

> "1943. You're Abraham Wald. The bombers come home with holes everywhere except the
> engines. Where does the armour go?"

Let them choose, then watch the red ghosts of the planes that never came back.

> "The engine holes aren't missing because engines don't get hit. They're missing
> because those planes went down. The same trap hires fund managers today: out of 1,024
> coin-flippers, one always has a perfect ten years."

## 1:55 The league table (40 s)

*Plate V.*

> "You're the health minister, and this is the front page. What do you do?"

After the skyline flies into the funnel:

> "District 177 is just small. The one real problem ranked 6th. The ranking hid it, and
> the funnel shows it."

## 2:35 Your record (25 s)

*Plate VII: the judge's own choices, marked, around a turning globe of ink.*

> "Every choice you made tonight, and the world it belonged to. The turkey, the
> Datasaurus and the doctor are waiting at the booth. Which world you're in, and how
> you draw it, decide half the answer."

Close: "It's one offline HTML file in three.js, built with Claude from two books, and
every number on screen is checked against them by a test."
