A thousand and one days

A turkey is fed every day for 1,000 days. Each day adds to its evidence. How sure should it be that it gets fed on day 1,001?

In Mediocristan, extremes fade back toward the average, so the past is a fair guide. In Extremistan, the one day that matters was never in the data.

Counting would have caught him

Between 1975 and 1998 a family doctor in Manchester, Harold Shipman, killed at least 215 of his patients. The deaths sat in plain sight, in ordinary records, in the tame world where statistics works.

Weight, height and death rates live in Mediocristan, where patient counting beats gut feeling. Wealth, markets and pandemics live in Extremistan, where the past can't warn you. Knowing which world you're in comes before any statistic.
