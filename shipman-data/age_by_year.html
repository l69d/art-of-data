Reading the plot

The horizontal axis is the actual date of death, not the year bucket, so points spread across each year by the day they fall on. The vertical axis is age in years; the three points near zero are infants who died on the wards at Pontefract.

Circled points are the fifteen murders Shipman was convicted of at Preston Crown Court on 31 January 2000. The Inquiry did not rule on those — the jury already had.

The gap at 1976

Shipman left the Todmorden practice in late 1975 after being caught forging pethidine prescriptions for himself, and spent 1976 out of general practice. He joined the Donneybrook House practice in Hyde in 1977 and set up alone on Market Street in 1992.

The last death here is Kathleen Grundy's, on 24 June 1998. Shipman was arrested that September when her daughter, a solicitor, questioned a forged will.

Method & caveats

Sex is taken from the honorific the decision uses for the person (Mrs, Miss, Mr) — unambiguous in every case here. Age, date and verdict are regex-extracted from the decision text, so treat them as good but not audited.

This is not the full picture: the Inquiry examined 887 death certificates and found at least 215 unlawful killings, but published individual decisions for only some of them. 27 further decisions are omitted here because they state no age or no date.
